## How to read this report

- **Ad counts are snapshots.** They reflect the active ads loaded on 6 Oct 2026. Ad Library loads results in pages, so treat counts as "at least this many".
- **"Started running" dates are the strongest public signal.** Ad Library in Bangladesh does **not** show spend, targeting, reach or results for normal ads. An ad still running after 30+ days is the best public hint that it's working.
- **Prices come from competitor websites or their ad copy** on the research date. They change often.
- Anything marked ⚠️ is inferred or partly verified.

## 1. Executive Summary

1. **Ostad is the market leader and the main threat.** It runs ~140 active ads in Bangladesh, about two dozen for its *Master AI Automation & Build AI Agents (No Code)* course (৳7,800, 34 live classes, Batch 13 starts **31 Oct**). Its oldest AI-automation ad has run since **5 Jul 2026** (3+ months), which strongly suggests the funnel converts.
2. **The market splits into three price bands:**
   - Cheap recorded n8n courses: ৳500–৳990 (SkillfoX, Next Digit Learner)
   - Mid-price ~৳4,000–৳5,000 (MSB Academy at ৳3,999, Codemanbd at ৳5,000)
   - Premium live bootcamps: ৳7,800+ (Ostad)

   **NextAI's ৳3,999 live cohort sits in the mid band**, and is cheaper than most *live* options.
3. **Almost every competitor sells "career + income."** They promise freelancing income, "earn in dollars," fear of being left behind, and job support. Almost no one sells **"a working system for a business."** That's NextAI's open lane.
4. **Fake-looking discounts are the norm.** MSB shows ৳15,000 → ৳3,999, SkillfoX ৳3,500 → ৳500, and Codemanbd "first 10 people" coupons. NextAI's no-strikethrough rule will stand out as honest. It must be backed by stronger proof, though, because competitors show student counts NextAI can't match yet.
5. **There are three funnel types:**
   - (a) Free demo class or workshop → course page (Ostad, Creative IT free classes)
   - (b) Messenger/WhatsApp direct (Codemanbd, Creative IT)
   - (c) "Get offer" → landing page → bKash/Nagad (Next Digit Learner, MSB, SkillfoX)

   NextAI's WhatsApp-first funnel copies (b), which is the most personal but the slowest to scale.
6. **Your Step 1 build is already free on YouTube.** Bangla YouTube tutorials on "Messenger auto-reply bot with n8n" have 50k–80k views each. NextAI must sell the **full chain** (lead → reply → CRM → WhatsApp handoff → weekly report) plus live guidance, not any single piece.
7. **Global players are already using "AI Employee" language in Bangladesh feeds.** Scale Certified sells an "AI Employee + Automation System" to agency owners, and Maker School promises "your first AI-service client." The phrase "AI employee" isn't unique, so it needs a Bangla, local-business demo to own it.
8. **The cohort start dates collide.** Codemanbd's batch starts 15 Oct, Ostad's Batch 13 on 31 Oct, and NextAI's on 3 Nov. Ostad's free demo classes run weekly, so its retargeting pool will compete with NextAI's whole launch window.

## 4. Direct Competitor Deep-Dives

### 4.1 Ostad — the benchmark to beat

**Offer**
- *Master AI Automation & Build AI Agents (No Code)*: ৳7,800, 17 weeks, 34 live classes plus weekly support classes, lifetime recordings
- 11 projects (lead generation, email support, voice agents and more), built on n8n, Make.com, Voiceflow and Claude/OpenAI
- Batch 13 starts **31 Oct 2026**
- Instructor: Shourov Barua (Ostad co-founder/COO) plus two TAs
- Job-placement support (CV/LinkedIn review, mock interviews)

**Meta ad strategy**
- **Volume and breadth:** ~140 active BD ads across all courses. Almost every course is now framed as "AI-driven" (UI/UX, DevOps, SQA, Digital Marketing).
- **Longevity:** The free weekly AI-automation workshop ad has run since **5 Jul 2026**. Course ads date from early Sep and get refreshed every few days. *Long-running plus frequently refreshed = a proven, scaled funnel.*
- **Format:** ~70% video; nearly all ads have multiple versions (dynamic creative or copy testing).
- **CTA:** Almost all **"Book Now"** (book a free demo class), not "Enroll."
- **Copy pattern:** Long Bangla copy with a week-by-week syllabus ("Week 1–3: …"), an outcome list ("you will be able to…"), and big social-proof numbers ("22,000 learners took these free demo classes").
- **Hooks seen:**
  - "Start a career as an AI Automation Expert (No-Code)"
  - "Want to earn by building AI agents without coding?"
  - "The best AI Automation course in Bangla"
  - Trend-jacking a new open-source AI agent project

**Funnel**
> Ad → **free live demo class** (Zoom; topic: "How to build your first AI agent and where to sell it") → phone verification and booking → course page (৳7,800, promo codes, countdown) → bKash/Nagad/card

**Strengths:** Huge brand, live format, a 3-month-proven ad funnel, big proof numbers, job support.

**Weak spots NextAI can use**
- **4-month commitment** at ৳7,800. That's heavy for a working freelancer or business owner.
- Taught by an edtech operator, not a team that ships client systems.
- Income- and job-heavy framing; broad project list, not one finished business system.

**What it means for NextAI:** Don't try to out-volume Ostad. Win on **faster, more focused, built by production builders, about half the price.** Expect Ostad's free-class retargeting to hit the same people during your launch.

## 8. Patterns Across the Market

### Hooks everyone uses
- **Career/income:** "start a career as an AI automation expert," "earn in dollars," "$1000+/month"
- **Fear of being left behind:** "what will your skill be worth in 2–3 years?"
- **No-code reassurance:** "without coding," "drag-and-drop"
- **Social-proof numbers:** "22,000 attended," "1,103 students," "100+ in 6 days"
- **Scarcity:** "first 10 people," "seats almost finished," countdown timers

### Hooks almost nobody uses (NextAI's open lane)
- **A business pain, shown on screen:** "your Messenger leads are dying"
- **One finished system, not a course list:** "you'll ship *this*"
- **Honest outcome framing:** skill, not income promises
- **Instructor as practitioner:** "we build these for clients — watch us build one"

### Funnel models

| Model | Who | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free demo class → course page | Ostad, Creative IT (free classes) | Builds trust at scale; strong retargeting pool | Needs a webinar engine and time |
| Messenger/WhatsApp direct | Codemanbd, Creative IT, ICT Bangladesh | Personal, high close rate on warm leads | Doesn't scale without a fast closer |
| "Get offer" → landing page → bKash | Next Digit Learner, MSB, SkillfoX | Self-serve, scales cheaply | Needs strong landing-page proof; low price |

**NextAI = WhatsApp direct.** That fits a ~90-seat cohort but depends on your 30-minute reply target. Competitors with free demo classes have a trust step you don't have yet. **Your founder videos and demo reels must do that job.**

### Creative formats
- Video dominates for leaders (Ostad ~70%, MSB 14/15). Static images are used for syllabus or price detail.
- Most leaders run **multiple versions per ad**, a sign they test copy and creative variations.
- Long Bangla copy with emoji bullets and week-by-week syllabi is the norm. NextAI **can't** publish a syllabus yet (not locked), so lean on the **5-step build** as the "syllabus substitute."

## 9. Opportunities & Threats for NextAI

### Opportunities
1. **Own "build a working business system" vs everyone's "learn a career skill."**
2. **Cheapest live option.** ৳3,999 live vs ৳5,000 (Codemanbd) and ৳7,800 (Ostad).
3. **Honesty as a feature.** No fake ৳15,000 price, no "$1000/month." Some buyers are tired of these claims.
4. **Business-owner and agency persona is under-served.** Only Idean Consulting and Creative IT's Chattogram ad speak to business tasks; most ads target students and freelancers.
5. **Faster commitment.** If the cohort is shorter than Ostad's 4 months, say so *once the syllabus is locked*.

### Threats
1. **Ostad's retargeting and brand** during 6 Oct – 3 Nov.
2. **Codemanbd's 15 Oct batch** can pull your warmest WhatsApp leads first.
3. **Proof gap.** Every competitor shows student numbers; NextAI has none yet.
4. **Price anchoring** by ৳500–৳990 recorded courses ("why pay ৳3,999?").
5. **"AI Employee" isn't unique.** Global sellers already use it.

## 10. Implications for the 3-Week Plan (input for Step 3)

These are hypotheses to test, not the plan itself.

- **Angle test for Week 1:** "Leads die in Messenger" (business pain) vs "Sell AI automation to clients" (freelancer). Competitors almost all run the freelancer/career angle, so the business-pain angle may be cheaper to stand out with.
- **Trust asset:** Make 1–2 founder reels **show the live build**. This replaces the free-demo-class step competitors rely on.
- **WhatsApp closer script:** Prepare answers for "Ostad / Codemanbd / ৳990 course is cheaper or has more students." Lead with live classes, a finished system and production builders.
- **Timing:** Launch before Codemanbd's 15 Oct start if possible, so you're in the conversation before their batch closes.
- **Monitor weekly:** Ostad's AI ads, Codemanbd, SkillfoX's ৳500 ad, and whether Programming Hero or Interactive Cares start AI ads.
