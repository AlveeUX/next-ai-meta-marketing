## 1. Strategy — Why This Plan Looks the Way It Does

### 1.1 Positioning: take the lane competitors left empty
Competitors sell **career and income**: "become an AI Automation Expert," "earn $1000/month," "job support." Nobody sells **a working system for a business**.

**NextAI's position:**
> *"আমরা ক্লায়েন্টদের জন্য AI employee বানাই — এবার আপনি নিজে বানাবেন।"*
> *("We build AI employees for clients — now you'll build one yourself.")*
> **শিখবেন না — বানাবেন।**

**Why:**
- It's the only claim backed by something real (NextAI ships these systems).
- It avoids the income and job claims we're banned from making.
- It sounds different from Ostad, MSB, Codemanbd and Next Digit Learner, which all sound alike.

### 1.2 Price: lead with "live," never with "cheap"
৳3,999 is the **cheapest live option** in the market (Codemanbd ৳5,000, Ostad ৳7,800). It's also the **same price as MSB's recorded course**, and far above ৳500–৳990 recorded courses.

- **Do:** "Live Bangla classes + same-day recordings + you finish with a working system — ৳3,999"
- **Don't:** "Cheapest," strikethrough prices or "৳10,000 → ৳3,999" *(unless the Founder confirms it really sold at ৳10,000)*

### 1.3 Funnel: Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA), and why it fits
- The Founder chose WhatsApp, and Bangladeshi buyers expect to ask questions before paying by bKash/Nagad.
- A WhatsApp chat lets the closer handle price, YouTube and trust objections live, which matters because **we have no testimonials yet**.
- Messages after an ad click are free for **72 hours**, so all follow-ups should happen inside that window.
- **Trade-off:** it only scales as fast as the closer replies. The 30-minute reply target is a **hard requirement**, not a nice-to-have.

### 1.4 Replace the trust step competitors use
Ostad's funnel runs through **free demo classes**: 22,000+ attendees claimed, and the workshop ad has run since July. We can't copy that machine in 3 weeks. We **can** borrow its core idea cheaply:
- **Founder-on-camera build reels** as ads (Weeks 1–3)
- **One free Facebook Live build session** (Week 2, organic, ৳0). It builds trust, gives us a warm audience to retarget in Week 3, and produces clips.

### 1.5 How Meta's delivery works in 2026, and what it means here
- **Creative is the targeting.** Meta's ad-matching system decides who sees an ad mostly from the ad itself. With a tiny budget, **broad targeting plus clear, specific creative** beats narrow interest stacks. The creative must "call out" the freelancer or the business owner by what it shows.
- **Creative variety counts.** Ads Manager added a **creative diversity** column (Aug 2026). Similar-looking ads get treated as one. Each test ad must look *visibly different*, not just have different text.
- **Placement exclusions were removed (20 Aug 2026).** You can't fully exclude a placement any more; you can only reduce its bid by up to 90% with a "placement value rule." Use this if Audience Network or low-quality placements bring junk chats.
- **Detailed-targeting exclusions aren't available** for new campaigns. You can't exclude "students" by interest. Handle the midterm-season undergrads with **age 22+ and creative that speaks to working people**.
- **Learning phase:** an ad set needs roughly 50 results a week to stabilise. At ৳200/day we won't reach that, so Weeks 1–2 read **directional signals**, not final answers. That's why the decision rules (Section 7) use simple thresholds.

## 4. Week-by-Week Plan

### Week 1 — Find the angle (Mon 12 – Fri 16 Oct)

**Budget:** ৳1,000 = **৳200/day for 5 days**, not ৳143/day for 7.
**Why:** a higher daily budget gets cleaner delivery and more data per day. It also leaves the weekend to analyse and build Week 2 creatives. Codemanbd's batch starts 15 Oct, so we want our message in front of people during their decision window.

**Structure:** 1 campaign → 1 ad set → **3 ads (one per angle)**
**Why one ad set, not three:** splitting ৳200/day three ways (~৳67 each) is below a useful level. One ad set with three very different creatives lets Meta show each ad to the people it fits. The prefilled codes still tell us which angle produces *paying* leads, not just cheap chats.

| Ad | Angle | Persona | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | Business pain | Agency / business operator | "Your Messenger leads are dying." Screen demo: a lead comes in → AI replies → logs to a sheet → hands off to WhatsApp → weekly report |
| **B1** | Sell a service | Freelancer (#1 buyer) | "Clients are asking for AI. Can you build it?" Show the finished AI employee as a sellable service; no income claims |
| **C1** | Builder proof | All | Founder to camera: "We build these for clients — now you'll build one yourself." Close with শিখবেন না — বানাবেন |

**What we learn:** which angle gives the **lowest cost per *qualified* chat**. Not cheapest clicks, and not cheapest chats.

**Weekend review (17–18 Oct):** fill in the scorecard (Section 7.1), pick the winning angle, and brief 3 new hooks for it.

### Week 2 — Fix the funnel (Mon 19 – Fri 23 Oct)

**Budget:** ৳1,000 (৳200/day × 5). *Optional:* +৳1,000 if Week 1 already produced a payment at ≤ ৳800 per enrollment.

**Structure:** same campaign, **new ad set** `W2_Winner_Hooks` → **winning angle × 3 new hooks** + keep the best Week 1 ad running as a control.
**Why:** Week 1 tells us *what to say*; Week 2 finds *how to open it*. In short-form video the first 2 seconds decide most of the result.

**Hook test examples** (if the business-pain angle wins):
- H1: Screen shows "Unread messages: 47" → "এই ৪৭ জন কাস্টমার আজ অন্য কোথাও কিনবে।" ("These 47 customers will buy somewhere else today.")
- H2: Founder: "আমাদের এক ক্লায়েন্টের অর্ধেক লিড রিপ্লাই ছাড়াই মরে যেত।" ("Half of one client's leads used to die without a reply.") **Only if this is a true client story.**
- H3: Fast 5-step build montage, 10 seconds, text-led

**Fix the closing side too.** Week 1 chats show where people drop off. Rewrite the WhatsApp script for the top 3 objections (likely price, YouTube and coding fear).

**Free Facebook Live — Thu 22 Oct, 9 PM (organic, ৳0)**
- Title: *"Live: ৪৫ মিনিটে একটা AI Employee বানাই"* ("Live: let's build an AI employee in 45 minutes")
- Founder builds Steps 1–2 live (lead → auto-reply → sheet). End with: "বাকি ৩টা স্টেপ ক্লাসে বানাবেন — WhatsApp করুন" ("You'll build the other 3 steps in class — message us on WhatsApp").
- **Why:** this copies the trust mechanism behind Ostad's proven funnel, at zero cost, and gives Week 3 a warm audience of video viewers to retarget.
- Promote it organically: Page post, founder profile, groups, a WhatsApp status the day before.

### Week 3 — Scale what works (Mon 26 Oct – Sun 1 Nov)

**Budget:** ৳3,000–5,000. **Start at ৳450/day** and increase **by 20–30% every 2–3 days** while the cost per enrollment stays inside the gate.
**Why gradual:** large jumps push the ad set back into learning and usually raise costs for a few days. We have no days to waste.

**Structure:**

| Ad set | Share | Audience | Ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| `W3_Prospecting_Broad` | **~70%** | Same broad 22–40 BD | Best 2 ads from Weeks 1–2 + 2–3 *new* variants of the winning angle (visibly different, to keep the creative diversity rating Medium/High) |
| `W3_Warm_Retarget` | **~30%** | Video viewers (50%+, last 30 days, FB+IG) + Page/IG engagers + Live viewers | Live replay clip (60–90 s), "What exactly will you build" walkthrough, FAQ objection reel (missed class → same-day recording; YouTube vs live), honest date reminder: "ক্লাস শুরু ৩ নভেম্বর" ("Classes start 3 November") |

- **Exclude existing buyers:** upload paid students' phone numbers as a Custom Audience and exclude it. Custom-audience exclusions still work.
- If the warm audience is under ~1,000 people, **merge it into one ad set** rather than starving it.
- **Full-Stack (৳1,999) and the Bundle (৳4,999) never appear in ads.** They're offered in WhatsApp only, per the brief.

**Close-out (Sat 31 Oct – Mon 2 Nov):**
- Shift most of the budget to warm retargeting.
- Closer messages every open chat from the last 72 hours with one honest final note: date, what they'll build, how to pay.
- No fake countdowns. If the 90-seat cap is real, show **actual seats left**.

## 5. Angles & Creative Direction (input for content planning)

| Angle | Says | Must show | Never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Business pain** | Leads are lost because nobody replies fast; an AI employee fixes that | The real 5-step system running on screen, with Bangla business examples (F-commerce, agency) | "Guaranteed more sales" |
| **B — Sell a service** | Freelancers can build and offer AI automation to clients | The finished system as a portfolio piece; the **"Built: [Project]" certificate with QR to the demo** | "Earn $X/month", "get clients guaranteed" |
| **C — Builder proof** | We ship these for clients; you'll learn from builders | Founder, real NextAI work, live Bangla class format, same-day recordings | "Best course in Bangladesh", "cheapest" |
| **Warm / objection** | Answers the 3 most common Week 1–2 objections | Missed class → recording; YouTube vs live build; coding level (honest) | Fake testimonials, invented student numbers |

**Format rules for all ads**
- 9:16 vertical first (Reels/Stories); 4:5 version for Feed if cheap to make
- Hook in the first 2 seconds; captions burned in (most people watch muted)
- 20–45 s for cold audiences; 60–90 s allowed for warm
- Every ad ends with the same action: **"WhatsApp করুন — ২টা প্রশ্নের উত্তর দিন, আমরা জানাবো কোর্সটা আপনার জন্য কিনা।"** ("Message us on WhatsApp — answer 2 questions and we'll tell you if the course fits you.") This is an honest, low-pressure CTA.
- Copy: short Bangla, 3–5 lines plus the 5-step list. Don't copy the competitors' wall-of-emoji style; looking different is the point.

**Meta policy guardrails** (also protect the account)
- No income or earnings claims. Meta treats these as misleading or get-rich-quick risk, and the brief bans them anyway.
- Don't assert personal attributes. Write "ফ্রিল্যান্সারদের জন্য" ("For freelancers") rather than "আপনি কি টাকার অভাবে আছেন?" ("Are you short of money?").
- No fake urgency, fake discounts or before/after income screenshots.

## 6. WhatsApp Sales Desk (where the sale actually happens)

**Speed rule:** reply **within 30 minutes** during campaign hours (suggested 10 AM – 11 PM). Pause ads outside closer hours if replies would be late; a lead answered 8 hours later is mostly lost.

### Flow
1. **Greeting (automatic):** thank them, set expectations ("2টা প্রশ্ন — তারপর সব details"; "2 quick questions, then all the details").
2. **Q1 — What do you do?** ① Freelancer ② Agency/business owner ③ Job holder ④ Student
3. **Q2 — Have you used any automation or no-code tool before?** Yes / No / Not sure
4. **Route:**

| Answer | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ①/② + Yes or Not sure | **AI Agents & Automation ৳3,999** | Core buyer |
| ①–③ + No, but motivated | AI course + honest note on the effort needed; Bundle ৳4,999 if they want foundations too | Partially suitable per brief |
| Can't afford ৳3,999 / total beginner | **Full-Stack ৳1,999** as an honest beginner path with an upgrade route | Downsell per brief |
| ④ Student in midterms / wants a job guarantee | Be honest: no job guarantee; invite them to the next cohort | Protect refunds and reputation |

5. **Payment:** bKash/Nagad number → they send the TrxID → **receipt within 1 hour** → cohort invite → kit.

### Follow-up cadence (inside the free 72-hour window)
- **+24 h:** answer their open question plus a short demo clip
- **+48–72 h:** one honest final nudge (start date, what they'll build)
- After that: label them "Lost – reason" and stop. Don't spam.

### Labels (WhatsApp Business)
`New` · `Qualified` · `Payment pending` · `Paid – AI` · `Paid – Bundle` · `Paid – FullStack` · `Lost – price` · `Lost – trust` · `Lost – time` · `Lost – other`

### Quick replies to prepare
Start date · What you'll build (5 steps) · Missed class → recording · Certificate ("Built: [Project]" + QR) · Payment steps · Coding required? · Refund policy · Can I sell this to clients? (yes, as a skill, no income guarantee) · YouTube vs this course

## 7. Measurement & Decision Rules

### 7.1 Weekly scorecard (per ad)

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Spend, impressions, CPM | Ads Manager | Cost of reach |
| **Hook rate** = 3-s video plays ÷ impressions | Ads Manager (custom column) | Does the opening stop the scroll? |
| **Hold rate** = ThruPlays ÷ 3-s plays | Ads Manager | Does the message keep attention? |
| CTR (link/all) | Ads Manager | Does the offer pull a click? |
| **Cost per conversation started** | Ads Manager | Cost of a chat |
| **Qualified rate** = Qualified ÷ chats | Tracking sheet (by ad code) | Is the ad attracting the *right* people? |
| **Cost per qualified chat** | Sheet | The real Week 1 winner metric |
| Paid enrollments, revenue, by product | Sheet | Money |
| **Cost per enrollment (CAC)** | Spend ÷ enrollments | The scaling gate |
| Median closer reply time | Sheet | Funnel health |

### 7.2 Kill / keep rules (judge an ad only after it has spent ≥ ৳250)

| Signal | Threshold *(starting assumptions — replace with Week 1 actuals)* | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate | < 20% | Re-cut the first 2 seconds; keep the message |
| Hold rate | < 15% | Message is unclear or too long; shorten |
| Cost per chat | > ৳40 | Pause the ad |
| Qualified rate | < 30% | Wrong people: tighten the creative's call-out (show business/freelance context) |
| Chats but no payments after ~15 qualified chats | — | Problem is in WhatsApp (script, speed, trust), **not** the ad. Fix the closing before adding spend |

*Benchmarks are planning assumptions for Bangladeshi CTWA edtech. Your own Week 1 numbers replace them.*

### 7.3 CAC gate for scaling (Week 3)

| Cost per enrollment | Zone | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ ৳600 (≈15% of price) | 🟢 Green | Increase budget 20–30% every 2–3 days |
| ৳600–৳1,000 | 🟡 Yellow | Hold budget; refresh creative; fix closing |
| > ৳1,000 (≈25% of price) | 🔴 Red | Cut back to the best ad only; diagnose before spending more |

*Ask the Founder to confirm or adjust these numbers (Section 2, decision #7).*

## 9. Reality Check — What This Budget Can and Can't Do

**Planning maths** (assumptions to replace after Week 1):

| Step | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp chat | ৳15–35 |
| Chat → qualified | 30–45% |
| Qualified → paid | 15–25% |
| **≈ Chat → paid** | **~5–10%** |
| **≈ Cost per enrollment** | **৳300–700** (optimistic) to ৳1,000+ (if trust is low) |

**Total media across 3 weeks:** ৳5,000–7,000 → roughly **5–20 paid enrollments**, depending on how well the funnel converts.

**What this means:**
- Paid ads alone will likely fill **10–20% of a ~90-seat cohort** at this budget.
- **That's fine for a first cohort.** The real goal of these 3 weeks is to prove a cost per enrollment and collect the first students (who become testimonials for Cohort 2).
- If Weeks 1–2 show a **cost per enrollment under ~৳800**, every ৳1 spent returns about ৳4–5 in fees. That's the moment to show the Founder the **Option C** case (up to ৳120,000 with CAC gates) **with real numbers**, instead of asking for it on faith.
- Organic must carry the rest: founder network, NextAI clients' teams, freelancer communities, the Live session and referrals from early enrollees.

**My recommendation as a senior marketer:** keep your ৳1,000 → ৳1,000 → ৳3–5k plan as the **base case**. Pre-agree with the Founder that if Week 2 ends in the 🟢 zone, Week 3 can go to **৳10,000–15,000**. That's still inside Option C, but unlocked only by data.
