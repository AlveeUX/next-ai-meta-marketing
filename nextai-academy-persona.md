> **Purpose:** Single source of truth for who we're talking to, how we sound, and what we never say — for the Cohort 1 Meta launch.
> **Source:** Founder intake (October 2026). Anything marked *Hypothesis* or *Unconfirmed* must be validated by Week 1 data or by the Founder.
> **Hero product on Meta:** AI Agents & Automation (৳3,999). Full-Stack (৳1,999) is a WhatsApp-only downsell — never a Meta hero.

## 1. Brand Persona — Who Next AI Academy Is

**One line:** A builder's academy run by a team that ships AI agents and automation in production — not a course company teaching AI literacy.

**Core line:** **শিখবেন না — বানাবেন।**

**Core promise:** You will build a working AI employee yourself:
**Lead → Reply → CRM → WhatsApp handoff → Weekly report**

| Trait | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Builder, not lecturer** | Show the system working. Demos before claims. |
| **Honest** | No guarantees, no fake discounts, no invented proof. Say "not locked yet" when it's not locked. |
| **Practical** | Talk about leads, replies, CRM, clients — not "the future of AI". |
| **Bangla-first, live** | Live Bangla classes + same-day recordings. Speaks like a senior colleague, not a guru. |
| **Confident, not hype** | Calm proof ("here's what you'll ship") over urgency tricks. |

**Face of the brand:** Adnan Arefin, Founder @ NextAI — on camera (founder videos + demo reels).

**Proof we *can* use today:** NextAI ships AI agents, automation and Astra/n8n systems. 5 demo reels and 3 founder videos planned.
**Proof we *cannot* use:** Student testimonials, success stories, public case studies — none exist yet.

## 3. Primary Persona — Rafi, the Freelancer (#1 buyer)

**Snapshot (hypothesis):** 24–32, works on Fiverr/Upwork or local clients — web, design, marketing or VA work. Earns in gigs, wants a higher-value service to sell.

**Core problem:** Has no sellable AI service. Clients are asking about AI; he can't deliver it.

**What he wants**
- A concrete AI service he can offer clients
- A demonstrable build to show in his portfolio/proposals
- Practical skill, not theory

**What he's thinking**
- "Everyone's selling ChatGPT courses — is this different?"
- "Can I actually build this, or is it just watching videos?"
- "Can I sell this to my clients after?"

**Hesitations → how we answer**

| Hesitation | Answer (honest framing) |
|---|---|
| Price (৳3,999) | You leave with a working AI employee you built — a portfolio piece you can demo. |
| "YouTube is free" | YouTube gives clips. This is a live Bangla cohort, practical kit, same-day recordings and a finished build. |
| Trust / no testimonials | Show the team's real production work (demo reels). Founder on camera. Don't fake proof. |
| "Will I earn from this?" | You'll learn the skill and how to sell AI automation as a service. No income guarantee. |
| Coding fear | Tools-based building; you need willingness to build. *(Exact coding level: unconfirmed.)* |

**Hook angles**
- "Your clients want AI. Can you build it for them?"
- Show the 5-step AI employee working end-to-end in a reel.
- Certificate that says **"Built: [Project]"** with a QR code to the live demo — proof he can show a client.

## 4. Secondary Persona — Nusrat, the Agency / Business Operator

**Snapshot (hypothesis):** 26–40, runs or manages operations at a small agency, F-commerce business or service business. Gets leads through Facebook/Messenger.

**Core problem:** **Leads die in Messenger.** No proper lead → reply → CRM → report flow. Follow-up depends on people remembering.

**What she wants**
- Automate replies and lead tracking
- Hand hot leads to a human on WhatsApp
- A weekly report without manual work

**Hesitations:** Time commitment, "is this for technical people?", trust.

**Hook angles**
- "How many Messenger leads did you lose this week?"
- Before/after: lead sits unanswered → AI replies, logs to sheet, hands off on WhatsApp, sends a weekly report.
- The build stack *is* her problem solved — show it, step by step.

## 5. Secondary Persona — Tanvir, the Career-Switcher / Digital Marketer

**Snapshot (hypothesis):** 23–30, early-career job holder or digital marketer who wants a more future-proof skill.

**Core problem:** Wants a buildable, demonstrable skill. Tired of theory-only courses.

**What he wants:** Something real to show — a project, a demo link, a certificate tied to a build.

**Hesitations:** Job-guarantee expectations, time, coding fear.

**Hook angles**
- "Stop collecting certificates. Build something you can show."
- **শিখবেন না — বানাবেন।**

**Watch-out:** This group overlaps with job-guarantee seekers. Never imply job readiness or placement.

## 6. Downsell Persona — Mim, the Genuine Beginner (WhatsApp only)

**Snapshot:** Student, beginner or early job holder with no/minimal coding. Clicked the AI ad, but AI feels too far — or ৳3,999 is a stretch.

**When she appears:** In the WhatsApp conversation, after the qualification questions surface a **budget** or **"I'm a total beginner"** objection.

**What she wants:** Foundations, a first build, a path toward AI later, maybe freelancing in future.

**Offer ladder (closer uses in this order)**
1. AI Agents & Automation — ৳3,999 (if she can stretch)
2. Bundle (AI + Full-Stack) — ৳4,999 (if she wants both foundations and AI)
3. Full-Stack Web & Scalable SaaS — ৳1,999 (honest beginner entry with a path to AI)

**Hesitations:** "Too cheap to be good", "Is it enough for a job?", "Do I need a laptop?", duration.

**Say:** Honest ৳1,999 beginner path · 100-day course · clear upgrade path into AI Agents.
**Never say:** Job-ready, 80% scholarship, "cheapest", or promote it as a co-hero in ads.

## 8. Buyer Journey & Funnel Touchpoints

**Main flow:**
**Meta ad → WhatsApp → 2-question qualification → bKash/Nagad → receipt → cohort invitation → kit**

**Downsell branch:**
**WhatsApp → budget/beginner objection → Full-Stack or Bundle offer → payment → enrollment**

| Stage | What the persona needs | Asset |
|---|---|---|
| Scroll | A problem they recognise (dead leads / no AI service) | Demo reel, founder hook video |
| Click | "This is real and built by builders" | Founder on camera, working system |
| WhatsApp | Fast, human answers to FAQs | Named closer, reply **within 30 min** in campaign hours |
| Pay | Easy local payment | bKash / Nagad, instant receipt |
| After pay | Confidence they chose right | Cohort invite + kit |

## 10. Messaging Guardrails

**Must communicate (AI ads)**
- শিখবেন না — বানাবেন
- Working AI employee
- Live Bangla classes + same-day recordings
- Approximately 90 seats *(only if it's a real cap)*
- Production builders

**Never communicate**
- Job or income guarantees
- Fake % discounts, fake original prices, strikethrough pricing
- Module or class-hour counts (syllabus not locked)
- "100-day" claim in AI ads
- "Cheapest"
- Fake scarcity or fake testimonials
- Full-Stack + AI as dual Meta heroes
- 80% scholarship (Full-Stack)

## 11. Open Questions for the Founder

These block final copy or targeting. Resolve before Week 1 ads go live where marked.

1. **Public course name** — "AI Agents & Automation" or "AI Agent & Automation Engineer"? *(Before Week 1)*
2. **Start date** — Is Tue, 3 Nov 2026 confirmed? *(Before Week 1)* — note: that's ~4 weeks from now, so a 3-week campaign ends right before the cohort starts.
3. **৳10,000 "course fee"** — Has the course ever sold at ৳10,000? If not, showing ৳10,000 → ৳3,999 risks breaking the "no fake original price" rule. *(Before Week 1)*
4. **90 seats** — Is this a real hard cap? If not, drop it to avoid fake scarcity. *(Before Week 1)*
5. **WhatsApp closer** — Who is it? 30-minute reply target needs a named person. *(Before Week 1)*
6. **Refund policy** — Yes/no and terms.
7. **Coding level** — What exactly does "willing to build" require?
8. **Age range** — Keep 22–40 as a test hypothesis; validate with Week 1 data.
9. **Full-Stack details** — Instructor, laptop requirement, syllabus.
